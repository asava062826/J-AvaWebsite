ABOUT

J-Ava is a tireless composer, animator, and programmer whose work reflects a vast skill set, and a desire to create human experiences and art. She has composed largely for various video game projects throughout the years, maintaining her skills as a programmer and animator in conjunction with being a composer. She has collaborated with friends for many different student projects. This work includes composing for SCAD student Kai Sulima’s upcoming Aria & Belle, an animated coming-of-age comedy series, and the score of the short film “Don’t Eat Me,” which was written in a single day. In addition to collaboration with others, she has also spearheaded her own animated shorts, composing the score for her personal films, The Butterfly and Golden Oboe. In the realm of video games, she has written an excess of music for her own personal project, Altuvia, which mixes together orchestral and chiptune sound palettes. In conjunction to these projects, J-Ava is studying as a sophomore at the Peabody Institute at Johns Hopkins University. There, she has gained technical proficiency in a myriad of different audio and game development softwares. She is very well versed in Logic Pro, and utilizes ProTools for mixing and mastering and Dorico for sheet music engraving. She primarily utilizes the game engine Gamemaker Studio 2 for producing games, while also working in Unity. She primarily utilizes the software Wwise for audio integration and implementation, and has worked extensively in this software creating adaptive and dynamic music systems for her video game projects. No matter what project she works on, J-Ava strives to combine technical polish and professionalism with her unbridled joy for creation.
